# Cartógrafo · geography duels you can play live with friends

[![Cartógrafo](images/cartografo.png)](https://cartografo.app)

**[cartografo.app](https://cartografo.app)** is a free geography game that runs in the browser, on phone or computer, with no download and no sign-up. Find countries, capitals and flags on the map against the clock, or take a quiz on rivers, mountains and population. A game takes about two minutes.

Play it in **[Spanish](https://cartografo.app/es)**, **[English](https://cartografo.app/en)** or **[Portuguese](https://cartografo.app/pt)**.

![Playing Countries of Europe](images/gameplay.jpg)

## Ways to play

- **Live with friends:** open a room, send the link (on WhatsApp, say) and up to 12 people play the same map at the same time, each on their own phone. Most points wins.
- **Challenge link:** play solo and send your score; your friend plays exactly the same game, in the same order, whenever they can.
- **Daily challenge:** the same map and order for everyone, once a day, with a [worldwide leaderboard](https://cartografo.app/en/leaderboard).
- **Solo:** beat your record. Each piece is worth up to 100 points: be quick, don't miss.

## Maps

30 maps, each with its own games (map, capitals, flags, quiz):

- **The world and every continent:** [world](https://cartografo.app/en/world), [Europe](https://cartografo.app/en/europe), [Asia](https://cartografo.app/en/asia), [Africa](https://cartografo.app/en/africa), [North America & Caribbean](https://cartografo.app/en/north-america-caribbean), [Central & South America](https://cartografo.app/en/central-south-america), [Oceania](https://cartografo.app/en/oceania).
- **Provinces, states and regions of 22 countries:** [Spain](https://cartografo.app/es/provincias-de-espana) (provinces and [autonomous communities](https://cartografo.app/es/comunidades-autonomas-de-espana)), [Portugal](https://cartografo.app/pt/distritos-de-portugal), France, Italy, Germany, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Austria, Poland, [the United States](https://cartografo.app/en/us-states), Canada, Mexico, Cuba, Colombia, Venezuela, Ecuador, Peru, [Brazil](https://cartografo.app/pt/estados-do-brasil), Bolivia, Chile, Argentina and Uruguay.

All maps: [cartografo.app/en/maps](https://cartografo.app/en/maps).

## Who makes it

I'm Oscar, from Tarragona (Spain). I love travelling and learning the geography of a place before I go. Playing with a friend to see who knew the map better, we looked for something quick to challenge each other live, each on our own phone, and found nothing, so I built Cartógrafo. [The full story](https://cartografo.app/en/about).

## Under the hood

A static Next.js site with live rooms on Cloudflare (Workers and Durable Objects). Maps from Natural Earth and Spain's IGN, capitals from Wikidata, flags from flag-icons and Wikimedia Commons. The game's source is not public.
