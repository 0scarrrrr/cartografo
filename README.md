# Cartógrafo · geography duels you can play live with friends

[![Cartógrafo](images/cartografo.png)](https://cartografo.app)

**[cartografo.app](https://cartografo.app)** is a free multiplayer geography game that runs in the browser, on phone or computer, with no download and no sign-up. Find countries, capitals and flags on the map against the clock, take a quiz on rivers, mountains and population, or travel back to Europe in 1914, Africa under the colonial empires, Asia after the Second World War or Latin America on the eve of independence. A game takes about two minutes.

Play it in **[Spanish](https://cartografo.app/es)**, **[English](https://cartografo.app/en)**, **[Portuguese](https://cartografo.app/pt)**, **[French](https://cartografo.app/fr)**, **[German](https://cartografo.app/de)** or **[Italian](https://cartografo.app/it)**.

![Playing Countries of Europe](images/gameplay.jpg)

## Ways to play

- **Live with friends:** open a room, send the link (on WhatsApp, say) and up to 12 people play the same map at the same time, each on their own phone. You see live how far ahead or behind the leader you are, and a room scoreboard keeps the wins and points across rounds.
- **Challenge:** play a game and send the link; your friends play exactly the same pieces, in the same order, whenever they can. Only each player's first try counts, and the ranking stays on the link. [How multiplayer works](https://cartografo.app/en/multiplayer-geography-game).
- **Map of the day:** the same map and order for everyone, once a day, with a [worldwide leaderboard](https://cartografo.app/en/leaderboard).
- **Solo:** beat your record. Each piece is worth up to 100 points: be quick, don't miss.

## Historical maps

Could you find Austria-Hungary, Eastern Rumelia or the Free City of Danzig? Twelve maps of the countries as they were, each with a countries game, a capitals game of the time (Kristiania, Constantinople, Kaunas, Bonn, Boma, Urga, Chuquisaca, Chongqing…) and a quiz; Europe in 1914 and 1989 also have a flags game. A button draws today's borders over the old ones.

- **[1880](https://cartografo.app/en/europe-1880):** after the Congress of Berlin. The Ottoman Empire still reaches the Adriatic; Bulgaria and Eastern Rumelia; Bosnia occupied by Austria-Hungary.
- **[1914](https://cartografo.app/en/europe-1914):** on the eve of the First World War. Four empires, and no Poland.
- **[1938](https://cartografo.app/en/europe-1938):** between the wars. Danzig, the Polish Corridor, Czechoslovakia whole, Lithuania with Kaunas as capital.
- **[1945](https://cartografo.app/en/europe-1945):** the end of the Second World War. Germany in four occupation zones; Poland moved west.
- **[1989](https://cartografo.app/en/europe-1989):** the end of the Cold War. Two Germanys, the Soviet Union, Yugoslavia, Czechoslovakia.
- **[Africa in 1914](https://cartografo.app/en/africa-1914):** the colonial partition. French West Africa, the Belgian Congo, German East Africa, Kamerun; only Ethiopia and Liberia independent.
- **[Africa in 1960](https://cartografo.app/en/africa-1960):** the year of Africa. Seventeen new countries in a single year; Algeria still French, Rhodesia and Nyasaland a federation.
- **[Asia in 1914](https://cartografo.app/en/asia-1914):** on the eve of the First World War. British India with Burma, the Ottoman Empire down to Mecca and Yemen, Nejd and Jabal Shammar before Saudi Arabia, Japan with Korea and Karafuto, Tibet and Outer Mongolia breaking away from China.
- **[Asia in 1924](https://cartografo.app/en/asia-1924):** after the Ottoman Empire. The French and British mandates, the Kingdom of Hejaz before Saudi Arabia, the new Republic of Turkey and Soviet Union, Tuva and Mongolia as people's republics.
- **[Asia in 1945](https://cartografo.app/en/asia-1945):** the end of the Second World War. Korea split at the 38th parallel, British India whole, Indonesia and Vietnam proclaiming independence, China's capital in Chongqing.
- **[Latin America in 1810](https://cartografo.app/en/latin-america-1810):** on the eve of independence. The viceroyalties of New Spain, New Granada, Peru and the Río de la Plata, Portuguese Brazil, and Haiti, already free.
- **[Latin America in 1825](https://cartografo.app/en/latin-america-1825):** the new republics. Gran Colombia, the Federal Republic of Central America, Bolivia with its Pacific coast, and Uruguay as Brazil's Cisplatine province.

The borders are built from today's countries and regions where the old border follows them, and from lines drawn by hand where it does not, good to about 10 km (colonial Latin America's inland borders, which only existed on paper, are approximate); every map is checked against a list of cities that must fall in the right state.

## Who makes it

I'm Oscar, from Tarragona (Spain). I love travelling and learning the geography of a place before I go. Playing with a friend to see who knew the map better, we looked for something quick to challenge each other live, each on our own phone, and found nothing, so I built Cartógrafo. [The full story](https://cartografo.app/en/about).

Ideas, bugs or a map you would like to see: **hola@cartografo.app**.

## Under the hood

A static Next.js site with live rooms on Cloudflare (Workers and Durable Objects). Maps from Natural Earth and Spain's IGN (the historical ones too, cut along the borders of the time), capitals from Wikidata, flags from flag-icons and Wikimedia Commons; the historical flags are drawn for the game. The game's source is not public.
